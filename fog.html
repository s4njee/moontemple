EXPERIMENT 07 / A LAMP ON A FOGGY NIGHT

Intothe fog.

A row of street lamps on a foggy night under the moon: the halo round each head, the cone of light under each shade standing in the fog, and the pools where they meet the wet road, with the fog drifting through the beams. Nothing is painted on: every pixel works out how much of the lamp's light the fog in front of it scatters towards you.

Light you can

01 / HALO

The air lights up.

On a clear night you only see a lamp's light where it lands. In fog, droplets along every line of sight scatter a little of it towards you, mostly forwards, so the air round the lamp glows and the halo is brightest close to the head.

02 / CONE

The beam stands up.

The shade lets light out only downwards, so only the fog inside its cone is lit. That cone stands in the air as a solid shape. The glow along each line of sight through it is worked out exactly, in closed form, not marched in steps.

03 / DRIFT

The fog moves.

The fog is two layers of noise drifting at different speeds, so the patches change as they travel through the beam. The lamp itself breathes very slowly, if at all: it never flickers, so the page stays calm to read.
