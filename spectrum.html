EXPERIMENT 01 / LIGHT OUT OF FOCUS

Slowspectrum.

A hundred points of light far behind a lens focused close, drifting upwards while the whole field turns round the colour wheel: red, then green, then blue, a full turn a minute. Move the pointer and the nearer lights move more.

THE PAGE IN FRONT / THE LIGHTS FAR BEHIND IT01 — A LENS FOCUSED CLOSE

WHERE THE DISCS COME FROM

Points of light,
out of focus.

01 / DEPTH

Blurred by distance.

The lens is focused half a metre away and the lights are between one and a half and thirty metres behind it, so each becomes a disc nearly the size of a point at infinity: the nearer ones a little smaller, rising faster and swaying wider.

02 / LIGHT

Each disc keeps its light.

A disc is the lens's aperture drawn at that light's blur, carrying exactly the light of the point it came from. Where discs overlap they add, which is what makes a crowd of them look photographed rather than drawn.

03 / COLOUR

One wheel, turning slowly.

Every light takes the same hue, give or take the spread, and the hue moves on in real seconds. Its brightness is evened out so the green third does not glare: it breathes, and never flashes.
