EXPERIMENT 03 / A CITY OUT OF FOCUS

Nightstreet.

Two lanes of traffic, rows of sodium lamps, neon signs and traffic lights, seen through an 85 mm lens at f/1.4 focused a metre and a half away, so every light is a soft disc of its own colour. Move the pointer down the page to pull the focus out along the street.

How it works ↓
THE PAGE IN FRONT / THE CITY BEHIND IT03 — AN 85 MM LENS AT F/1.4

WHERE THE DISCS COME FROM

A street,
out of focus.

01 / FOCUS

Pulled by the pointer.

The top of the page focuses a little under a metre away, the bottom a hundred and twenty metres down the street, and the lens eases between them like a focus ring. Lights near the focus shrink to points; the rest swell into discs.

02 / LIGHT

Every light its own disc.

Headlights come towards you on the left and tail lights leave on the right, under sodium lamps every twenty-two metres. Each is a disc the size of its blur carrying exactly its light, so the far ones are small, bright and many.

03 / ROAD

A wet road.

Rain on the tarmac mirrors every light below the road's surface, dimmer and farther away, so it blurs differently from the light itself. Dry the road and the reflections go.
