EXPERIMENT 09 / ONE BEAM, ONE PRISM

Light,divided.

A sheet of white light crosses a dark room, passes through a glass prism and comes out the other side as every colour it was made of, red bent least and violet most. Every ray is traced through the glass one wavelength at a time, so the band is exactly as wide as the glass makes it.

WHERE THE COLOURS COME FROM

01 / DISPERSION

Glass bends blue more.

Light slows down in glass, and short wavelengths slow down most, so each colour turns by a slightly different angle at each face. Through two faces of a prism the difference adds up, and white light comes out as a fan of colours.

02 / GLASS

The glass sets the width.

Crown glass spreads the colours a little, heavy flint glass about twice as much, and diamond more again. Switch the glass and the band on the wall narrows or widens, as it did for Newton in 1666.

03 / LAMPS

Not every light is white.

A hot filament gives every colour, so its spectrum is a continuous band. A gas lamp gives only a few: hydrogen four lines, mercury five, sodium one bright yellow pair. The prism sorts whatever goes in.
